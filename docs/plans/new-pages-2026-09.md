## Hardware safety (binding for every slice and lane)

Unchanged from the programs before this one: tests never write to TotalMix ports 7001–7010 or to the real sACN bridge (10.1.0.1); a scratch engine runs with a scratch `SSE_APP_DATA_DIR`, `SSE_AUDIO_SIMULATED_INPUT_MODE=1`, `SSE_SAFE_START=1` and a bridge port other than 38201; nothing starts an engine against `%APPDATA%\ExEd Studio Control Native`; the live database is read only from a copy with its `-wal` and `-shm`; TotalMix FX and Companion stay running and untouched; the live app is closed only with the operator's go-ahead, by ending its shell, and reopened from its folder with no `SSE_*` variable, fullscreen on display 3; `scripts/native-package.mjs` runs only with `release\native\windows` moved aside (keep-and-restore); never `--include-release`; never `npm audit fix`; Playwright and a qualification lane never run together; the qualification lanes run in CI only — Slice SF found them unsafe on the workstation even with the live app closed (the Setup/Support lane probes off-host addresses, this PC has a second default route through 10.1.0.1, and a lane step arms the light outputs), so they stay off it until the operator decides otherwise with a safety design of their own.

The six rules of D15, agreed 2026-09-24, in force before the first line of camera or prompter code:

1. **The BGH1s (network).** A camera address exists only once the operator types it into Setup; new saved data holds none, so a scratch or test run contacts nothing. Test builds refuse to connect to any camera address that is not on this PC, the way they drop every TotalMix datagram. Tests talk to a fake BGH1 on 127.0.0.1, on a port chosen at random.
2. **The Pocket 6K Pro (Bluetooth).** Tests, lanes and scratch runs never use Bluetooth; they run the simulated camera. Only the live app uses Bluetooth. Pairing happens once, in Setup, with the operator present (the camera shows the PIN).
3. **Pictures.** Tests never open the DeckLink, the Cam Link or any camera stream; they get a still test picture, and the screenshots mask the live picture as they mask the Console's meters.
4. **The prompter.** Only the live app draws on the Prompter XL. Tests and lanes draw the prompter into an ordinary window, never onto a real screen.
5. **Checks against the real cameras** happen only with the operator present and no camera recording, and put back every setting they touched.
6. **Learning the BGH1's protocol.** If Panasonic has not published it, watch LUMIX Tether talk to one BGH1 while the operator uses it — listen only, never send — with the operator's go-ahead at the time.

The workstation facts these rules rest on (read without changing anything, 2026-09-24): the main camera is a Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera 6K Pro (HDMI → DeckLink 8K Pro; set on the body or from the iPad app Bluetooth+ over Bluetooth; about 3 m from the PC, whose Intel radio does Bluetooth); two Panasonic BGH1 on PoE on the office network at 172.16.16.85 and 172.16.16.30, set from LUMIX Tether over the network — one HDMI → SDI → DeckLink, one HDMI → Cam Link 4K; vMix normally, OBS while the vMix licence is expired; the ATEM is not used; Companion 5.0.6 holds only the Stream Deck, X-keys and Studio Control connections; the Prompter XL is a USB-C screen Windows has seen (`Prompter XL`, not connected on 2026-09-24), run today by a home-built program.

**2026-09-25 — the flash test waits for its selection** (branch `fix/lighting-flash-test-waits-for-selection`, cut from `main` at `e8d43c5`; a task the operator started from the chip raised while S2 was checked). `frontend/app/tests/lighting-rig-actions.spec.ts` "The page shows each flash start and end by itself: Identify, then Find" failed now and then on the workstation, before this program too (3 of 5 runs at `359ee4a`, 4 of 5 at `e8d43c5`, 2 of 20 and 4 of 30 in repeated runs), while CI passed it on every run. The failing line was 149 — the check after **Find**, which has the same words as the one after Identify. Five failing traces showed the same thing at the Find click: Key at 100 percent and Back off, so Find had run over the selection as it was before Shift+Enter added Back. The cause is the test: its `selectFixture` pressed Enter or Shift+Enter and returned at once, and the Find click followed about 2 ms later; the selection reaches the screen a render after the key (the store asks the fixture double, the reply comes back, the page draws it), and on a fast, idle page the browser handles the click first. Anything that slows Playwright or the machine hides it — a trace, one more round trip, load, even the page's CPU slowed sixfold (the click then waits behind the pending render) — which is why CI and loaded runs passed. The product is right: a person cannot click within a render of the key.

Fix: `selectFixture` waits for the marker to say it is selected (`aria-pressed` `true`); every one of its eleven calls selects a light. Under the same conditions the case with the old helper failed 4 of 30 runs and with the new one passed 30 of 30; with the page's CPU slowed sixfold (CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 6) 10 of 10; the whole file three times, 30 of 30.
