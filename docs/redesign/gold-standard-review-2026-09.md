## 1. Method

- Every fixture in `fixtures.json` was rendered in Chromium at `2560x1440`, `1920x1080` and `1280x800`, in Studio, Graphite and Bone, with the clock frozen at the visual-review fixture time. Per capture: a full-resolution screenshot, scroll metrics, a census of every visible text node (size, family, weight, transform, tracking, colour, box), every pointer target (box, disabled state), corner radii, clipped and off-viewport elements, and console errors.
- Contrast is **pixel-sampled**: for each text node the rendered pixels just inside its box give the background the eye sees (gradients, glass and meter fills included), the text colour is composited over it, and the WCAG ratio is computed. The first DOM-walk estimate over-counted on gradient backgrounds and was discarded.
- Chrome geometry (header, top bar, footer, rail, inspector, badge slot) and "calm" metrics (running animations after settle, box-shadows, backdrop blurs, gradients, uppercase share, weight ≥ 700) were measured on the twelve representative fixtures at the three viewports.
- Interactive states were driven with Playwright: shortcut guide, palette, restart and close dialogs, keyboard focus, hover, arming (snapshot recall), the not-verified disabled state, EQ tab, Sync, patch overlay, overflow and context menus, DMX monitor, 1280 drawer and tooltip, planning board / time report / new project, Setup support mode and the sample-data dialog.
- The 1:1 crops and every screenshot were inspected by eye at native size; the in-app browser pane was not used for judgement.

Cost scale used for ranking: **Critical** = can cause a wrong action during a take or hides a hardware truth; **High** = misleads or slows the operator in a normal session, or breaks a documented mode; **Medium** = costs glances or coherence; **Low** = polish.
